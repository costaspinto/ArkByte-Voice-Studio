# Code of Conduct

ArkByte Voice Studio welcomes respectful, constructive collaboration.

- Be respectful and professional.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Share reproducible technical details when reporting problems.
- Do not submit recordings, personal data, credentials or private voice samples without the owner's explicit permission.
- Do not use the project to impersonate or clone a voice without authorization.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for harassment, privacy violations or misuse of voice-cloning features.
